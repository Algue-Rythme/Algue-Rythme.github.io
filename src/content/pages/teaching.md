**Practical sessions.** At Université Paul-Sabatier: Advanced Algorithmics (M1 SID), Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (L3), Computer Science (MAG1), and Machine Learning and Deep Learning (M2 SID), from 2020 to 2024. At ISAE-Supaéro: Machine Learning for the Data Science MSc (2020/2021, 2022/2023).

I was also a teaching assistant at the [Reinforcement Learning Virtual School](https://rl-vs.github.io/rlvs2021/index.html) (2021), and an animator of the *Girls can code!* summer camp at ENS de Lyon (2017–2019).
